# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🧾 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Superstore Sales Dashboard** built using Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends. The dashboard provides actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing categories and sub-categories
* Evaluate regional and segment-wise contributions
* Understand shipping trends and delivery efficiency
* Track monthly sales and profit patterns over time

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales data, including:

* Order details (date, ship mode)
* Product categories and sub-categories
* Sales, profit, and quantity
* Customer segments
* Geographic regions and states

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* Data visualization techniques
* Basic data modeling and aggregation

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

* **Total Sales:** 1.6M
* **Total Profit:** 175K
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 22K
* **Average Delivery Time:** 4 days

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🏷️ Category Performance

* Office Supplies leads in total sales
* Technology shows strong revenue potential
* Furniture has relatively lower performance

### 📦 Sub-Category Highlights

* Phones are the top revenue generator
* Chairs and Binders also contribute significantly

### 🚚 Shipping Analysis

* Standard Class is the most used shipping mode
* Faster shipping options are less preferred

### 🌍 Regional Insights

* West region contributes the highest sales
* South region shows the lowest performance

### 👥 Customer Segments

* Consumer segment dominates overall sales
* Corporate and Home Office segments present growth opportunities

### 📅 Time Trends

* Sales and profit vary across months (2019–2020)
* Seasonal patterns indicate potential for demand forecasting

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Improve performance in underperforming regions (e.g., South)
* Focus on high-revenue categories like Technology
* Optimize logistics for Standard Class shipping
* Expand Corporate segment through targeted strategies
